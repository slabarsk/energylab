03 / BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Simple math. Clear answers.

01

Turn power into energy

kWh = (W ÷ 1000) × hours

A 1,000 W device running for 2 hours uses 2 kWh of energy.

02

Scale it to your period

Period kWh = daily kWh × days

Using 2 kWh a day adds up to 60 kWh over 30 days.

03

Find the cost

Cost = kWh × electricity rate

At an example rate of 3 ₺/kWh, 60 kWh of energy costs 180 ₺.
